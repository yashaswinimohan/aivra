# AI Academy
The AI Academy project was an initiative at San José State University to develop an AI-powered learning platform that enables students to access AI courses, complete assessments, track learning progress, and eventually earn certifications. The goal was to create a scalable platform that could support AI education across different departments while providing an engaging learning experience for students.

As the Technical Program Manager, I was responsible for coordinating the entire program from planning through MVP delivery. I worked closely with faculty, engineering, design, and student teams to define the product roadmap, gather requirements, prioritize the backlog, and manage project execution. I facilitated Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives while tracking milestones, risks, dependencies, and overall project progress.

One of the biggest challenges was balancing the different priorities of stakeholders. Faculty wanted rich learning features, engineers focused on technical feasibility, and leadership wanted an MVP delivered within the semester. To address this, I translated business goals into user stories and technical requirements, prioritized features based on business value and engineering effort, and maintained clear communication through regular status updates and sprint reviews.

The MVP included features such as a course catalog, course detail pages, learner onboarding, quizzes, progress tracking, feedback collection, and analytics. I also established project governance by maintaining project plans, RAID logs, sprint documentation, and delivery dashboards. We successfully prepared the platform for the pilot launch of the AI Fundamentals course, with a target of achieving 70%+ student engagement. The project strengthened my skills in technical program management, stakeholder communication, roadmap planning, Agile delivery, and cross-functional leadership.
